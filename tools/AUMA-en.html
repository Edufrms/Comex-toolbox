1. What this directory or organiser provides

AUMA is the association of the German trade-fair industry. Its fair finder helps identify exhibitions, particularly in Germany, by sector, place and dates. AUMA is useful during B2B market entry because the underlying event documentation may include exhibitor and visitor figures; the specific basis of each figure still needs checking.

Important: event details change by edition. Validate all dates and participation terms with the actual organiser before committing funds.

2. Step-by-step research workflow

Identify the industry and German buying regions

Start with customer profile, sectors and regional commercial clusters instead of selecting fairs solely by headline attendance.

Locate the relevant event in Fair Finder

Use industry and location filters where available; review alternative terms because different organisers classify adjacent technologies differently.

Read the organiser's edition information

Check current date, venue, exhibitor profile, visitor origin and eligible participation formats.

Look for audited statistical evidence

Where available, examine FKM-certified figures and their edition, definitions and category coverage; not every statistic or fair is certified.

3. Realistic professional example

A cosmetics supplier from Spain wants to reach German distributors and private-label manufacturers. It uses AUMA to shortlist beauty and packaging-related exhibitions, checking which events actually attract procurement managers and manufacturers. It then reviews published exhibitor origins, travel accessibility, the event's current programme and measurable meeting objectives. A technically relevant fair may outperform a much larger general consumer event.

Decision rule: select a fair for accessible relevant buyers and a workable meeting strategy, not simply because it appears near the top of a directory.

5. Interactive next-step adviser

This is a planning aid, not a forecast of sales or the organiser's confirmation.

6. Participation checklist

  • Buyer segment and product scope defined
  • Current edition verified with organiser
  • Statistics and reporting year checked
  • FKM status verified where applicable
  • Travel, stand and follow-up budget assessed

Record a dated evidence file for each shortlisted event: official website, edition, location, audience profile, participation budget, likely buyer meetings and follow-up plan.
