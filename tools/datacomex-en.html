1. What DataComex provides

DataComex is Spain's Secretary of State for Trade platform for international goods trade statistics, including Spanish customs data and EU member-state data originating from Eurostat. It can be used to compare merchandise imports and exports by product, partner and period, including relevant Spanish regional breakdowns.

Declared tradeTrade declared to customs authorities is not necessarily identical to estimates of total merchandise trade; check which dataset you selected.
Regional breakdownsProvince-level reporting does not prove the precise manufacturing location of every exported product.
DataComex is not a named directory of foreign buyers. Aggregated statistics cannot identify specific importer contacts.

2. Step-by-step query

Define the research question

Example: how did Spanish wine exports to the United States change across comparable full years?

Choose the dataset and flow

Open the reporting or download interface and select goods trade, exports and the relevant territorial scope.

Specify product and partner

HS heading 2204 covers wine of fresh grapes and specified grape must. Check the available level of product detail, then select the United States as partner.

Keep the query reproducible

Record years, value and weight units, nomenclature version and extraction date. Check metadata for provisional data and series coverage.

4. Calculator: trade value per tonne

Enter value and tonnes covering the same product, destination and period. The quotient is not a transaction selling price.

5. Common pitfalls

Assuming preliminary figures are final

Check coverage, revision date and reporting status.

Equating province with production site

Territorial trade statistics do not prove where every unit was made.

Mixing Customs and Eurostat series

Document whether the series refer to declared trade, total estimates or European trade data.

Using operator counts as buyer directories

Enterprise statistics have their own definitions and are not a customer contact list.

6. Sources and next action

Document product code, flow, partner, territory, units, retrieval date, full-year periods and value/weight differences. Then cross-check international demand patterns.
