1. What the tool covers

Rules of Origin Facilitator, developed by the International Trade Centre and partner organisations, helps users research preferential trade agreements and product-specific origin provisions. It supports analysis but does not automatically certify a company's goods as originating.

Always confirm current legislation and official sources before relying on a tariff or origin outcome.

3. Practical scenario

A manufacturer assembles an industrial product using imported components and hopes to export under a preferential agreement. The product-specific rule might require a change in tariff classification, a manufacturing operation or regional value threshold. Packaging or dispatch alone may not suffice. Without a bill of materials and production records, claiming preferential origin would be unsound.

This scenario explains the research method; it does not assert current tariffs or product compliance.

4. Interpret the results

Preferential origin is not necessarily the seller's nationality. The applicable rule can involve tariff-shift tests, regional value, processing requirements or combinations. Check insufficient operations and documentary obligations.

The interactive guide is educational, not a customs decision.
