1. What Export Potential Map actually measures

Export Potential Map is a free tool from the International Trade Centre (ITC). Its model combines an exporter's supply capacity, import-market demand and bilateral ease of trade to highlight possible opportunities. It is not a forecast of orders for your company.

Export potentialA model-based estimate of potential trade for an exporter, product and destination under the ITC methodology.
Untapped potentialThe estimated portion of potential not reflected in observed trade. This is not a guaranteed additional sales pipeline.

Use it to decide which markets deserve further commercial investigation; it does not establish pricing, certifications, buyers, margins or distribution capacity.

Check methodology: the estimates depend on reference periods, data inputs and product classifications. Record the period and definition you used.

2. Guided research workflow

Identify the exact product

Do not mix cocoa beans with cocoa butter or processed chocolate. Check the appropriate HS coverage first.

Select exporter and product

Open the official ITC portal, set the exporting country and find your product; controls may move as the interface evolves.

Review destinations and untapped opportunity

Record estimated potential, observed exports and the unexploited gap, rather than copying only the top country.

Validate commercial feasibility

Cross-check demand history using Trade Map, market-access rules using Market Access Map, and real logistics quotations.

4. Market-prioritisation helper

This qualitative helper does not reproduce the official ITC calculation. It suggests the next due-diligence task after identifying a prospective destination.

This does not turn estimated potential into guaranteed revenue.

5. Four pitfalls to avoid

Untapped potential means signed orders

It is a model estimate, not verified demand from specific customers.

The highest ranking is the optimal market

Regulatory compliance, freight, competition and margin may make it unsuitable.

Similar product names imply the same category

Trade classifications matter; a different HS code can change the results.

The model replaces field research

Interview buyers, check historical flows and compare entry requirements.

6. Sources and next decision

Prepare a shortlist showing product code, reference period, ITC estimate, import trends, duty, key competitors, landed costs and distribution channels. Validate the top two markets before committing resources.
