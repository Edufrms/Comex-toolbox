How to use Export Potential Map to select export markets

A practical guide to turning estimated export potential into a shortlist of markets worth investigating.

1. What is Export Potential Map?

Export Potential Map is a tool from the International Trade Centre (ITC) that allows you to discover products and markets with high export potential, using trade, tariff, and supply and demand data.

  • Explore export diversification opportunities
  • Identify products with high untapped potential
  • Based on robust and transparent methodology
Note: It is especially useful for SMEs, governments, and trade promotion agencies.

2. How to access

  1. Visit exportpotential.intracen.org/en
  2. No registration is required to use most of its features
  3. You can change the language (available in English, French, and Spanish)

3. Search by country

From the main screen, choose an exporting country to see:

  • Its main export opportunities
  • Potential markets by product
  • Data on demand, supply, and geographical proximity
Tip: You can filter by categories such as food, textiles, machinery, etc.

4. Search by product

Select a specific product to visualize:

  • The countries with the highest import potential
  • The untapped potential
  • Existing competition in the target market
Important: Use the search bar with keywords or HS codes (Harmonized System).

5. Opportunity explorer

Functionality that allows you to identify country-product combinations with the greatest potential:

  1. Select an exporting country
  2. Observe products with high untapped potential
  3. Explore emerging markets for each product

6. Interpretation of results

The graphs show:

  • Export potential (maximum estimated value)
  • Current actual exports
  • Untapped potential
Note: Indicators such as stability, global demand, and ease of access are also presented.

7. Practical example

Export of cocoa from Colombia

  • Select Colombia as the exporting country
  • Search for "cocoa" or enter the HS code
  • Check the destinations reported by the tool; do not assume any country is the top opportunity without consulting the live results
  • Compare the current exported value with the estimated potential
Tip: You can export the data or generate graphs for presentations.

8. Final tips

  • Check the tool regularly for updates
  • Combine it with TradeMap for a complete analysis
  • Include the results in your internationalization strategy

9. Understand potential, current trade and the untapped gap

The International Trade Centre (ITC) Export Potential Map uses a model combining the exporting country's supply capacity, destination demand and bilateral ease of trade. It is neither a sales forecast nor a directory of prospective buyers.

Export potential is a model-based estimate under the indicator's assumptions. Current exports provide an observed trade benchmark. Untapped potential captures the estimated opportunity not yet realised. Even an attractive gap must be tested against production capability, competition, distribution and regulatory requirements.

Interpretation: do not present untapped potential as guaranteed extra revenue. Check the reference period, product coverage and methodology before quoting figures in a business plan.

10. Worked research scenario: Colombian cocoa

Imagine a Colombian company assessing destinations for cocoa beans. This is a research exercise, not a live ranking or a claim about which countries currently have the highest potential.

  1. Choose Colombia as exporter and match the precise product classification; distinguish cocoa beans from paste and butter.
  2. Record the potential estimate, observed exports and untapped amount for the destinations shown by the platform.
  3. Use Trade Map to check whether imports and competitive supply patterns support the initial shortlist.
  4. For two shortlisted markets, consult Market Access Map and request real freight quotations.

The preferred market is not necessarily the one with the largest estimated gap: compliance, a competitive landed price and a workable sales channel all matter.
