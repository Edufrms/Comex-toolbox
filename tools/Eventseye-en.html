1. What this directory or organiser provides

EventsEye is an international directory of exhibitions, trade shows and other professional events. Its value is broad discovery by sector, country or calendar. It should be treated as a discovery layer: event details, dates and the commercial scope of each edition need confirmation at source.

Important: event details change by edition. Validate all dates and participation terms with the actual organiser before committing funds.

2. Step-by-step research workflow

Search from the target market

Start with an export destination or strategic region and select the relevant industry or adjacent categories.

Use date and city filters

Narrow to feasible travel dates, avoiding major conflicts with launch schedules, public holidays or production obligations.

Inspect the event profile

Identify the organiser, exact exhibition dates, intended professionals and available links to further information.

Confirm direct sources and prepare an agenda

Verify the current edition through the organiser, then identify target exhibitors, distributors or potential buyers for meetings.

3. Realistic professional example

An exporter of construction and site equipment wishes to develop Morocco. It searches EventsEye for construction exhibitions in Morocco, checks dates and organiser information for a shortlist and reviews whether events target contractors, equipment distributors or consumers. The team then requests organiser information and contacts potential partners before booking travel. It does not assume every construction fair attracts machinery buyers.

Decision rule: select a fair for accessible relevant buyers and a workable meeting strategy, not simply because it appears near the top of a directory.

5. Interactive next-step adviser

This is a planning aid, not a forecast of sales or the organiser's confirmation.

6. Participation checklist

  • Sector and buyer segment aligned
  • Country and dates meet commercial objectives
  • Organiser and venue confirmed
  • Exhibitor or visitor evidence reviewed
  • Meetings and post-fair follow-up planned

Record a dated evidence file for each shortlisted event: official website, edition, location, audience profile, participation budget, likely buyer meetings and follow-up plan.
