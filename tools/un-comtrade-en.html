1. What UN Comtrade Plus offers

UN Comtrade brings together official trade statistics reported by economies and territories. The Plus interface supports goods trade research by reporter, partner, flow, product and period. Available breakdowns depend on what reporting authorities submit.

ReporterThe economy submitting the statistics; it is not necessarily your location.
PartnerThe trading partner referenced in the declaration, interpreted under the reporter's statistical definitions.
Official versus analytical: the platform distinguishes official submitted figures from certain estimated or analytical outputs. Always label the kind of data you use.

2. Build a bilateral query

Choose the classification version

Select an appropriate HS or SITC revision. Product definitions can change across revisions.

Select flow and reporter

To investigate shipments from Côte d'Ivoire to Spain, start with Côte d'Ivoire as reporter, exports as the flow and Spain as partner.

Specify product and period

HS heading 1801 refers to cocoa beans, whole or broken, raw or roasted. Choose a reporting year with data available and record the units.

Check mirror statistics

Repeat the query with Spain as reporter and imports from Côte d'Ivoire. Compare the two perspectives without forcing the figures to match.

Analytical insight: a mirror-data gap is a prompt for methodological investigation, not an invitation to overwrite the reported figures.

4. Mirror-statistics difference calculator

Enter two comparable positive reported values to calculate the relative gap. The tool does not adjust CIF/FOB values.

5. Four reasons to check the metadata

Missing reporting periods

Missing data is not zero trade; check release dates and availability.

Different HS versions

Product groups may not be strictly comparable across revisions.

CIF versus FOB

Import and export statistical values may use different valuation conventions.

Official versus estimated series

Comtrade analytical tools and estimated datasets need to be identified explicitly when cited.

6. Sources and next step

Save reporter, partner, flow, product, revision, period, units, retrieval date and data provenance. Investigate large differences through reporter metadata before drawing commercial conclusions.
