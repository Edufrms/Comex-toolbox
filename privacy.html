ComexToolbox privacy policy

Text last reviewed: 21 November 2026.

This policy applies to comextoolbox.com. The FairAgenda Android app has a separate privacy policy.

1. Controller and contact

The controller named in the site's legal notice (Spanish) is Eduardo Fernández. For privacy enquiries or requests to exercise your rights, email edufernandezramos97@gmail.com. The available identification details are provided in the legal notice.

2. Information processed and purposes

  • Comments: when you submit a recommendation, your chosen name and comment are stored using Google Firebase/Cloud Firestore for private review by the site operator; they are not published on the website. Do not include third-party personal data or confidential information.
  • Enquiries: information you send by email is used to respond to and manage your request.
  • Analytics and advertising: the site includes Google Analytics and AdSense technologies. Depending on their configuration and applicable consent, cookies, identifiers and browsing information may be processed.
  • Technical information: hosting and service providers may process technical data required to deliver, protect and diagnose the website, depending on their configurations.

3. Legal grounds

Recommendations and enquiries are processed to receive and manage messages submitted voluntarily by visitors. The legal basis considered applicable to reviewing and answering these submissions is the operator's legitimate interest in managing suggestions and requests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), taking account of the rights of the people concerned. Using the directory does not require submitting a recommendation. Optional analytics is enabled on the basis of consent requested through the analytics panel (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR); it can be withdrawn using analytics settings. Advertising-related processing is governed by the applicable advertising consent choices.

4. Access to submitted information and providers

Submitted names and comments are not displayed publicly on ComexToolbox. Messages are intended for private review by the site operator and are not included in a public listing. Comments are stored by Google's Firebase service. Google also provides Analytics and AdSense when enabled. See Google's privacy policy. Use of technology providers may involve processing or international transfers subject to each service's contractual terms and safeguards.

5. Retention

Recommendations and enquiries are retained for as long as necessary to review them, respond and manage related requests. They are then deleted once there is no longer a purpose justifying their retention, without prejudice to any applicable legal retention obligations. The form does not offer automated self-service deletion, but you can request erasure through the contact address. Measurement data, consent preferences and technical logs may have separate retention periods depending on browser and provider settings.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics supports website measurement and AdSense may be used for advertising. Continuing to browse must not be treated as automatic acceptance of non-essential cookies or identifiers. Where required, visitors should have a valid way to accept, reject and change their choice before non-essential storage or access occurs.

Analytics preferences: on pages using the analytics panel you can accept or reject Google Analytics before it loads, and change your choice through the “Analytics settings” button. Your choice is stored locally in your browser. Withdrawing consent stops subsequent Analytics loading after the page refreshes. This control does not manage advertising consent.

Advertising: AdSense code is included on the website, but displaying ads depends on account approval and configuration. Advertising consent is separate from the analytics panel and is managed through the consent tools configured for AdSense when applicable.

7. Your rights

As applicable, you can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Email edufernandezramos97@gmail.com to submit a request. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

8. External websites and video content

ComexToolbox links to independent third-party tools and resources that have their own privacy practices. Embedded tutorial videos may contact their providers when loaded.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when services, purposes or contact details change. Its review date appears at the beginning of this document.
