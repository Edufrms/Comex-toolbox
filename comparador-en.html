Three common international trade decisions

Trade Map, DataComex or UN Comtrade?

Trade Map offers international trade flows and partner trends. DataComex provides detailed Spanish trade statistics. UN Comtrade lets analysts cross-check country reports and mirror statistics. They need not compete: many projects use all three in sequence.

TARIC, Market Access Map or Access2Markets?

TARIC presents measures relevant to EU customs imports. Market Access Map supports international tariff comparisons. Access2Markets brings together tariffs, requirements and practical information for EU-related trade. Product classification and origin still require careful verification.

Which trade fair should you attend?

AUMA specialises in the German fair industry, while 10times, EventsEye and TradeFairDates help discover events across countries. After finding a candidate, confirm the current edition, buyer profile, costs and attendance conditions with its organiser.

How to read the results

No automatic winner is assigned. Tools differ in underlying sources, models, geographic coverage and terms of access. Start with a clear question and verify current information in each provider's official service.
